# Analyze Product

Turn an idea into an evidence-aware product recommendation. Do not manufacture market facts or user evidence.

## Workflow

1. Inspect supplied research, analytics, feedback, repository behavior, and business constraints. Label every material statement as fact, inference, assumption, or unknown.
2. Define the target segment, triggering situation, job to be done, pain, current alternative, and why the problem matters now.
3. Map stakeholders, primary journey, frequency, severity, willingness or incentive to change, and adoption friction.
4. Evaluate solution value, usability, technical feasibility, business viability, security/privacy exposure, and operational cost.
5. Identify competitors or substitutes only from provided or verifiable evidence. Compare outcomes and tradeoffs, not feature counts alone.
6. Form testable hypotheses and rank uncertainties by impact and evidence gap.
7. Propose the smallest discovery activity or product slice that resolves the highest-risk uncertainty.
8. Recommend `proceed`, `proceed with conditions`, `research first`, or `do not proceed`, with explicit reasoning.

Use [references/product-analysis.md](references/product-analysis.md) for a formal analysis artifact.

## Output contract

Include problem framing, users, evidence, alternatives, opportunity, assumptions, risks, hypotheses, success metrics, recommendation, and next validation step. Keep solution details provisional until the problem and outcome are credible.

Apply `facilitate-product-decision` when evidence leads to a material proceed, research-first, target-user, outcome, or investment choice. Analysis may recommend; the human owns `D2` product judgment and every `D3` authority decision when HITL is enabled.
