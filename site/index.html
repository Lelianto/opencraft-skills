Why it matters

AI can write code.
A product needs judgment.

A working screen is not the same as a useful product. Real delivery means knowing whose problem matters, choosing what not to build, designing every failure state, protecting sensitive data, and knowing exactly what “ready” means.

OpenCraft turns that judgment into a repeatable workflow your AI assistant can follow, while keeping the important decisions visible to you.

The complete journey

From uncertainty to a launch you can trust.

Each stage answers a plain-language question and leaves evidence for the next stage.

Separate what you know from what you assume. Understand the people, their current alternatives, the opportunity, and the evidence needed before investing.

  • Clear target user and real-world problem
  • Evidence, assumptions, risks, and unknowns
  • A proceed, research-first, or stop recommendation

Turn the validated opportunity into a readable product agreement: outcomes, journeys, permissions, requirements, success measures, edge cases, and explicit non-goals.

  • A build-ready product requirements document
  • Stable requirement IDs and acceptance criteria
  • Shared language for product, design, and engineering

Design the journey, content, interactions, and technical foundation together. Give the product a recognizable point of view without sacrificing clarity or accessibility.

  • Original design grounded in product meaning
  • Mobile behavior composed like a real app
  • Architecture, data, permissions, and recovery
  • Threats and security controls mapped early

Break the product into small, demonstrable slices. Every task has boundaries, dependencies, risks, and a clear way to prove it is complete.

  • Vertical slices instead of disconnected layers
  • Small tasks an agent can complete safely
  • A readiness gate before implementation begins

Build real behavior through tests, connect the complete journey, and diagnose failures from their root cause rather than layering on speculative fixes.

  • Test-backed behavior and working integrations
  • Responsive, accessible, touch-first interfaces
  • No placeholder core flows or fake completeness

Review what was built against what was promised. Test the complete journey across roles, devices, permissions, failures, sensitive data, migrations, and recovery paths.

  • Independent specification and code review
  • End-to-end, security, privacy, and data tests
  • Honest records of passes, failures, and gaps

Prepare the exact release, observation, and recovery plan. Deployment remains permission-gated, and production feedback becomes evidence for the next iteration.

  • Go, conditional-go, or no-go decision
  • Migration, smoke tests, monitoring, and ownership
  • Abort thresholds and a rehearsable rollback path

Product craft

Not another
AI-looking product.

OpenCraft rejects design that looks polished but says nothing. Originality must come from the people, language, data, and rituals of the product's real domain.

Reject by default

  • Fake metrics and testimonials
  • Interchangeable SaaS hero copy
  • Decorative gradients and glow
  • Card walls without hierarchy
  • AI features without a real job

Build instead

  • A clear product point of view
  • Truthful, domain-specific language
  • One or two signature ideas
  • Mobile journeys designed for touch
  • Real states and connected behavior

Common questions

Plain answers.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The workflow is designed to explain decisions in product language. Your coding agent handles repository details while keeping consequential choices, risks, and evidence visible to you.

Does OpenCraft choose my technology stack?

No. It reads and respects your existing repository first. For a new product, it chooses the simplest architecture supported by the requirements rather than imposing a fashionable framework.

Will it deploy automatically?

No. OpenCraft can prepare the complete deployment and rollback plan, but production deployment and other consequential external actions require explicit permission.

Does it replace designers, engineers, or security specialists?

No. It gives AI agents a stronger working method and makes their output easier for people to inspect. Human judgment remains essential for product decisions, risk acceptance, and production authority.

Can I use only one skill?

Yes. Invoke a focused skill for one stage, or use $ship-web-product to orchestrate the complete lifecycle.
